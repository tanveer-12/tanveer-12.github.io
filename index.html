Who I'm building toward

I'm Tanveer - an MS Computer Science student at WPI (AI concentration) and a Graduate Research Assistant at the RoboCare Lab. Across research and industry, the same problem kept finding me: agents that work beautifully alone fall apart the moment you put two of them in a room together.

So I'm aspiring to grow into a multi-agent system architect - someone who designs the coordination layer underneath agentic AI, not just the prompts on top of it. That's why I built AgentStateLib, a Python library that gives multiple agents a shared, typed state (SharedState) and a simple graph router (AgentGraph), so agents trade structured StatePatches instead of raw strings.

I believe in learning from first principles - whether that's engineering Pepper the robot's conversational pipeline at senior centers, training a DQN agent from scratch, or writing my own conflict-resolution logic for concurrent agent state.
